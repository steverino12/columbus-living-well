Mobility & the Future · Robotaxi Tracker

Where robotaxis run today, and when Ohio could follow

Tesla Robotaxi today

Tesla lists service in Austin, Dallas, Houston, Miami, Orlando, and Tampa, and Not a Tesla App reported on Oct. 5, 2026, that service also covers San Francisco. Rides in the Cybercab itself are available only in Austin so far.

Ohio status: not yet

  • No Ohio city has been announced.
  • Ohio has no legal path to paid driverless rides. ODOT/DriveOhio said in September 2026 that a law change would be needed.
  • Zoox is testing in Columbus with safety drivers; no service has been announced.
  • No Columbus service zones, depots, or charging hubs have been announced.

The Ohio Robotaxi Checklist

  • A bill allowing driverless commercial rides is filed
  • The bill passes
  • Tesla names an Ohio city
  • A charging or depot site appears in Ohio
  • Public rides begin

For landlords: EV charging as an amenity

A Level 2 charger is an amenity tenants value today, with or without robotaxis. Property owners with surplus parking may also want to watch for fleet charging and storage demand if Ohio opens up.

Sources: Tesla Robotaxi, Not a Tesla App, DriveOhio.
