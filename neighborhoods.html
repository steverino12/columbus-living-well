Neighborhood Spotlights

Three addresses worth knowing

Short North

The Short North is an arts district along High Street, just north of downtown, known for its galleries, restaurants, boutiques, and monthly Gallery Hop. It's close to the Greater Columbus Convention Center and the North Market, and it's mostly mid- and high-rise apartments plus historic rowhouses.

German Village

German Village is a historic neighborhood just south of downtown, with 19th-century brick homes and brick streets. It's listed on the National Register of Historic Places, and The Book Loft and Schiller Park are among its landmarks.

Dublin

Dublin is a suburban city northwest of Columbus, known for its Historic Dublin district and Bridge Park development along the Scioto River, the Dublin City Schools, and the annual Dublin Irish Festival.
