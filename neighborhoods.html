Neighborhood Spotlights

Four addresses worth knowing

Short North

The Short North is an arts district along High Street, just north of downtown, known for its galleries, restaurants, boutiques, and monthly Gallery Hop. It's close to the Greater Columbus Convention Center and the North Market, and it's mostly mid- and high-rise apartments plus historic rowhouses. Read the full Short North renting guide for transit, parking, voucher ZIP notes, and landlord–tenant checklist items.

German Village

German Village is a historic neighborhood just south of downtown, with 19th-century brick homes and brick streets. It's listed on the National Register of Historic Places, and The Book Loft and Schiller Park are among its landmarks. Read the full German Village renting guide for Commission exterior rules, GV permit parking, COTA lines, and 43206 voucher notes.

Dublin

Dublin is a suburban city northwest of Columbus, known for its Historic Dublin district and Bridge Park development along the Scioto River, the Dublin City Schools, and the annual Dublin Irish Festival. Read the full Dublin renting guide for city-versus-mailing-address checks, school boundaries, short-term rental limits, the new COTA Line 1 service, and 43016/43017 voucher notes.

Clintonville

Clintonville is a leafy, mostly residential neighborhood in north-central Columbus centered on North High Street, with older homes and doubles on ravine-lined side streets, COTA Line 2 on High, and Whetstone Park and the Park of Roses at its heart. Read the full Clintonville renting guide for the Clintonville Area Commission’s zoning role, Zone In on High Street, transit, and 43202/43214 voucher notes.
